Frequently asked questions about DearGift

What is DearGift?

DearGift (deargift.love) is a platform for creating personalized digital gift pages. Choose a template, add photos, a message and music, preview it, then share the private link or heart-shaped QR code.

Is DearGift free?

DearGift offers both free and premium templates. Template prices and optional photo, music, QR and duration charges are shown before you confirm the gift.

What makes the 3D Little Gift (Nov 19 Men’s Day & Every Occasion) template special?

It combines 7 interactive 3D scenes in a single private link: a tear-off calendar marking a special date (such as Nov 19 Men’s Day, a birthday or an anniversary), glowing particle text, an everlasting 3D rose bouquet, a 3D photo globe, a wax-sealed love letter, a secret scratch card and love-counter fireworks.

Do I need coding skills to create a romantic webpage?

No. The interactive effects are ready to use. Choose a template, enter your content, upload photos, select music and review the live preview before creating the link.

Which occasions does DearGift support?

The collection includes International Men’s Day (19/11), Teachers’ Day (20/11), Valentine’s Day, birthdays, Women’s Day (8/3 & 20/10), Christmas, anniversaries, love confessions, good-luck gifts, lucky wheels and playful quizzes.

What is a heart-shaped QR code?

A heart-shaped QR contains the link to your digital gift. The recipient scans it with a phone camera to open the experience; you can also download the QR image to send online or print on a physical card.

Does the recipient need an app or account?

No. The recipient can open the link or scan the QR on a phone or computer. If the creator enables a password, the recipient only needs the shared code.

Featured experiences include 3D Little Gift · Nov 19 Men’s Day & Every Occasion (7 scenes: tear-off calendar, 3D rose bouquet, photo globe, love letter, secret scratch card & fireworks), Valentine Messenger, Galaxy Love Viewer, 3D Galaxy Love, Small Gift 3D WebGL, animated birthday letters, surprise birthday cards, a love bus, lucky wheels and playful quizzes. Each template keeps its own content structure and interaction instead of using one generic form.
